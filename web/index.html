Would another build have hit harder?

Paste a match ID. The page loads every player's items from the Deadlock API, puts one player's build against an enemy's build at any point in the match, and tests which builds would have dealt more damage.

Find match IDs on the in-game match history, Statlocker or the Deadlock API.

Best overall builds

Builds ranked by how much damage they deal before dying against the typical real build of every hero at the same net worth. The enemy fights back with double damage (two players on you), never dies, and uses its real items, so health, resists, lifesteal, stuns and silences count. Counterplay is on: targets walk out of Alchemical Fire after 2 s, items the sim overrates compared with real matches are scaled down, and Refresher-style cooldowns are only ready in some fights. Movement items (Stalker, Majestic Leap, Arcane Surge, Warp Stone, Enduring Speed...) add their real chase and escape value: how much more often Phantom+ holders take part in their team's kills and how much less often they die, at the same hero and net worth. Phantom and up is the standard rank and refreshes first after each patch; higher ranks and all ranks follow.

Full game builds

One build that grows from 0 souls to 50k net worth, scored at 3k and 6k (early game) and at 10k, 18k, 27k, 37k and 50k. The buy order starts from an empty build. Every stage keeps what you already bought (upgrades credit their components, and an item is only sold when that clearly pays, at half its cost back), and every stage is scored with the overall test: damage dealt before dying against every hero's typical real build at that net worth. The path is chosen for its average stage score with the weakest stage counted twice, so no part of the game is left weak. The buy order shows when each slot crosses an investment threshold; the 4,800-soul spike is the big one.

Most damage per soul

For each hero and net worth, the item builds that deal the most damage to a training dummy, found by searching from the most popular real build of Phantom and up players, at their aim (75th percentile ranked), the site's standard. The dummy never cleanses or shoots back, so these builds skip defense. It does walk out of Alchemical Fire after 2 s unless the hero stuns, roots or slows it, so the fire only pays off on heroes that can hold a target in it; see Best overall builds for builds that hold up in a fight.

Counter items

Which defensive item helps most against a given enemy hero. Each hero's most popular real build gets the item added on top and is scored with the overall test: damage dealt before dying against the enemy's typical real build at the same net worth, with sustain, movement, parries and counterplay on. Next to the sim value: how much more often real players buy the item when that hero is on the enemy team, and whether buyers win more than players who bought another defensive item of the same price at the same time.

What changed

Every time the searches rerun (after a game patch or hotfix, newer real player builds, or a change to the combat model), the recommended builds can move. Each run below lists why it ran and, for every hero and net worth, the items that left and joined the best build and how its number moved: damage before dying for the best overall build, dummy damage by 15 s for the best raw damage build. Builds whose items stayed the same are listed only when their number moved 5% or more.
